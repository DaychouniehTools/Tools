Private account sign-in

Enter the email and password provided by the shop. The catalogue allows up to three customer sessions at one time.

Shop owners use the same sign-in. Owners do not count toward the three-customer-session limit.

Privacy notice: the shop records the account name, email address, and IP address when an owner or customer accesses the private catalogue. IP access logs are automatically deleted after 24 hours.

Shop owner access management

Create customer accounts with email and password. Set the access period in hours or days. A maximum of three customer browser sessions can be active at once; owner sessions are separate.

The password is sent securely to the authentication provider and is never saved in the customer list. Share the temporary password with the customer privately.

Import the private product catalogue

Choose the local catalog.js created by your importer (or a JSON product array). Product photos are uploaded separately to private storage; they are not published with the public website.
